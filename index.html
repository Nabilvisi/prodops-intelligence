EVALUATION FRAMEWORK (HALLIBURTON CONSULTING METHODOLOGY)

12-parameter screening rubric & weights

Scored on a 1 (Worst) to 5 (Best) rubric. Dimension balance: Subsurface & Reservoir 45%, Wellbore Integrity 35%, Operations & Commercial 20%. Hard knockouts apply to severe casing or barrier collapse, toxic HSE risks, or orphaned facility networks.

Tier Thresholds: Tier 1: Quick Win / Surface ($\ge 75$ score) · Tier 2: Medium Re-entry / CT / Reperforating ($55–74$ score) · Tier 3: Complex Rig Workover / Sidetrack ($35–54$ score) · Excluded: HSE / Integrity / Facility Knockout.

DRAFT SOW / TOR

Rigless well diagnostics support

Objective

Provide fit-for-purpose well diagnostic and readiness services to support an engineering decision on a proposed rigless intervention.

Scope & activities

Review available well data; prepare site readiness and HSE documents; execute approved diagnostic runs if mobilization is authorized; hand over calibrated logs and interpretation.

Technical requirements

Provide personnel competency records, equipment calibration records, method statement, execution plan and contingency response criteria.

Deliverables / acceptance

Pre-job program, HSE readiness pack, daily reports, calibrated acquisition files, depth-reference QC, final technical summary, punch-list closeout.

Exclusions & dependencies

No production uplift guarantee, well access approval, barrier verification, logistics allocation or intervention authorization is presumed.

This is a fictional procurement exercise. Actual SOW/TOR requires asset-specific engineering endorsement, vendor qualification, applicable SCM procedures and formal procurement approvals.
