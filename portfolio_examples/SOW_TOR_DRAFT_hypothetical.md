Review available production, completion and well history; prepare approved method statement, HSE readiness plan, mobilization schedule, diagnostics and reporting. No execution is permitted without proper field authorization.

Personnel competency, calibrated equipment, integrity/barrier reviews by authorized specialists, risk assessment, contingency plan, depth QC and documented evidence retention.

## Dependencies / Exclusions

Demonstration only. PTK-007 and internal SCM rules require independent review by competent operator/SCM personnel.
